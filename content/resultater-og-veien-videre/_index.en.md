The pilot is under way and ends in December 2026. There is no recommendation yet. This page summarises where the work stands, based on the draft mapping report (23 October 2026).

## Findings so far

- **No common solution:** municipalities, county authorities and institutions create their own fictitious numbers, without a common format, and the numbers cannot follow the person onwards. See [Use cases and user needs](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/brukstilfeller-og-brukerbehov/).
- **The National Population Register has no temporary identity,** and fictitious numbers do not give an eID. The transition to an official number is done manually. See [In Norway](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/naasituasjon/i-norge/).
- **The scale is considerable:** 18,000 S numbers for 102,000 new students in 2025, and 1.3–1.8 per cent of pupils in primary, lower secondary and upper secondary education without a national identity number (fødselsnummer).
- **Other sectors have their own solutions** that are hard to transfer. See [Other sectors](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/naasituasjon/andre-sektorer/).
- **Only Finland has a separate education ID,** and even there merging of duplicates is manual. Denmark solves the problem in the population register with administrative CPR numbers. See [Other countries and the EU](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/naasituasjon/andre-land/).
- **New European rules** (the EUDI Wallet and the Skills Portability Act) will require automatic linking of diplomas to the right person for up to 45 years, also for persons without a Norwegian ID. See [Future requirements](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/loesningsvalg/framtidige-krav/).

## The way forward

The pilot shall assess the [alternatives](/en/pilotering/pilot-5/loesningsvalg/alternative-loesninger/), complete the legal memo and make a recommendation, with proposals for what should be raised with the Ministry of Education and Research, what can be studied further in SAMT-BU, and what should be clarified with the Norwegian Tax Administration.
