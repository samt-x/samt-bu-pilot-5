The pilot has not assessed the alternatives yet. The draft mapping report (23 October 2026) points out five candidates and proposes criteria for assessing them. The alternatives do not exclude each other.

## Candidates

| Alternative | In brief | Model |
|---|---|---|
| 0 The current situation continues | The baseline for comparison. What happens if nothing is done, also when new number series are introduced in 2032 | |
| 1 Separate education ID in a separate register | A separate register with its own assignment and administrator, which requires a legal basis and must be seen together with Feide | Finland |
| 2 Extended use of the National Population Register | An ordinary D number is assigned early and kept, so no transition needs to be automated. Requires changing the conditions in the National Population Register Regulations (folkeregisterforskriften) | Denmark |
| 3 Cross-sector auxiliary number | Reuse of, or coordination with, the health sector's common auxiliary number, made nationally unique and given a legal basis | The health sector |
| 4 Person-centric identity without a new register | Feide translates between identities, without a new person register. Does not meet the need for statistics | Sweden (eduID) |

A step-by-step approach is also possible, where some things are done within the current legal basis while the legislative work runs in parallel. Finland phased in its scheme gradually from 2018 to 2023.

## Criteria

Proposed criteria, which the pilot shall weight before the alternatives are assessed:

- coverage of persons without an official ID
- lifelong duration
- national uniqueness
- possibility of automatic transition to an official ID
- privacy
- legal and technical feasibility
- time to effect
- cost
- transfer value to other sectors

## The question to the National Population Register

A key question is whether the National Population Register (Folkeregisteret) can be the home of temporary identities, and whether the transition to an official ID can be automated. The answer largely determines which alternative is realistic. It is known in the sector that the Norwegian Tax Administration (Skatteetaten) is to study a third identity type, but this has not been confirmed with a mandate or timetable. The pilot is in dialogue with the Norwegian Tax Administration.
